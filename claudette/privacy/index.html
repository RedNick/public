Claudette privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Claudette is a chat app for Wear OS watches, with a small companion app for Android phones. It is published by Nicolas Redfern ("we", "us"). Claudette is an independent project. It is not made, endorsed or supported by Anthropic.

In short

We run no servers and collect nothing automatically. Your chats go straight from your watch to Anthropic using your own API key. The only thing that ever reaches us is a report you choose to send by email.

What stays on your watch

  • Your Anthropic API key. It is encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore.
  • Your chats. The last 50 conversations are kept so you can reopen them.
  • Your settings, such as the chosen model and whether replies are read aloud.
  • Reports you make. If you use Report response, the reply, the question that prompted it, the model and your chosen reason are saved on the watch. See "Reporting a response" below.

What is sent to Anthropic

When you ask a question, the app sends your question and the earlier messages of that chat from your watch directly to Anthropic's Claude API over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. It authenticates with your own API key, so the request is made under your own Anthropic account. Anthropic's handling of that data is described in Anthropic's privacy policy and the terms of your Anthropic account. We never see these requests or Claude's replies.

Claude's replies are generated by AI and can be wrong, so check anything important.

Voice input and reading aloud

  • Voice input uses your watch's built-in speech recognition. The app receives only the resulting text; it does not request microphone access and never receives the audio. How the recognition service handles audio is covered by that service's own privacy policy (on many watches, Google's).
  • Read aloud uses your watch's built-in text-to-speech engine.

Reporting a response

If you tap Report response, the watch sends the report to your paired phone, and the phone app shows a notification that opens a draft email addressed to us in your email app. The draft contains the reply, the question that prompted it, the model and your reason. Nothing is sent unless you review the draft and press send yourself, and you can edit or delete any of it first. If you do send it, we receive that email, including your email address, and use it only to look into the problem. We delete reports once they are dealt with.

The phone app

The companion phone app installs the watch app and sends your API key to it. The key you type is sent to your paired watch through the Wearable Data Layer, which uses Google Play services and the encrypted link between your phone and watch. The phone app does not store the key and does not connect to the internet. When you report a reply it shows a notification, and the report is held only in that notification until you open or dismiss it.

What we do not do

We have no accounts, no servers, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. We do not sell or share your information. The only information we ever receive is an email report that you choose to send.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state (watch), so it can reach Anthropic's API.
  • Notifications (phone), to tell you a reported reply is ready to email.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Long-press a chat in History to delete it.
  • In Settings, Delete all chats removes every chat and every saved report.
  • In Settings, Remove key deletes your API key from the watch.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.
  • Data held by Anthropic is managed through your Anthropic account.

Children

Claudette is not intended for children, and you need your own Anthropic API key to use it.
